# Research Methodology: LLM Efficiency Review

This systematic review focuses on **Resource-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT)** techniques (LoRA, QLoRA) specifically for deployment on edge devices.

### Research Questions:
1. What is the optimal quantization bit-depth for maintaining 90%+ accuracy on mobile ARM architectures?
2. How do Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning methods impact battery consumption vs. full-model fine-tuning?

### Objective:
To bridge the gap between high-performance AI and real-world mobile deployment, ensuring secure and efficient AI models can run locally on user hardware.
